# Commissioner setup inventory

MFL's broad setup center informs coverage but not presentation. Football groups responsibilities into scannable cards with readiness state and direct settings routes. General, divisions, franchises, memberships, salary cap, roster limits, and imports have first-class route architecture. Remaining areas show a concrete data contract and next milestone.

Unresolved rules are deliberately not guessed: contract-year cap (120 or 130), IR maximum (5 or 15), offensive-flex QB eligibility, defensive-flex eligibility, dual-position treatment, and the independent scoring provider.
